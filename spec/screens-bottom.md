# Screens: Bottom

Every screen in the bottom's view of Ember for Phase 1. The bottom view should feel warmer and lighter than the Top's: fewer controls, gentler copy, and more room to reflect. It also has to work through a guest link for people who haven't installed the app.

## The bottom's loop

&#91;embedded content: bottom's loop · 11 steps\]

The bottom's attention is needed before and after the scene, never during it. Each debrief and feelings log feeds their profile, which they own and can update anytime.

## Navigation, onboarding and the guest link

The bottom's tab bar has five places: **Home, Connections, Scenes, Profile, Me**. Onboarding shares its first two screens with the Top's (O1 welcome, O2 privacy setup), then branches.

| ID | Screen | What it's for | Key elements | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BO1 | Accept invite | Join a Top who invited you | The Top's name and a one-line note from them; what Ember is in two sentences; privacy promise; Connect button | BO2 |
| BO2 | Your signals | Set how you say stop, slow and keep going | Traffic light words pre-filled; hand sign choice; optional custom word | BI1 |

**Guest link.** A person without an account opens a link from their Top and lands in the same intake screens in the browser, with nothing to install.

| ID | Screen | What it's for | Key elements | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | Guest welcome | Explain what this is before asking anything | Who invited them; what the Top will and won't see; how long it takes; a note that answers are encrypted | BI1 |
| G2 | Guest done | Close the loop | Confirmation that the Top has their answers; option to create an account to keep and edit their profile; SceneVibe, check-ins and debriefs arrive later by text or email link | none |

## Intake

Every path starts with a short set of essentials that the safety floor needs, then lets the bottom choose how much more to say. Progress saves automatically, so intake can be finished over several sittings.

| ID | Screen | What it's for | Key elements | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BI1 | Essentials | What every Top must know, whatever the path | Signals (if not set); experience level; how easily they speak up; body notes and injuries (body map tap); triggers and overwhelm plan (optional, with example chips); emergency contact; substance policy checkbox | BI2 |
| BI2 | Choose your path | Pick how to share desires | Three cards: Quick (word groups), Map (desire map), Detailed (every activity); each with time estimate and an info icon; can do more than one | BI3, BI4 or BI6 |
| BI3 | Heat, Hold, Flow | The quick path | Three screens, one per group; eight one-word icons each with info icons; pick one or two; skip a group | BI5 or BI7 |
| BI4 | Desire map | Place one dot | The map with its four quadrants; drag a dot; quadrant name and description update as the dot moves | BI5 or BI7 |
| BI5 | Desire decoder | Clarify a loaded choice | Appears only after a choice with several meanings; "what does this do for you?" with four to six icon answers; skip | BI7 |
| BI6 | Detailed | Every activity, one category at a time | Category screens (bondage, sensation, impact, touch and intimacy, penetration); yes / maybe / no per item; intensity slider on yes; body map for impact areas; marks; words welcome and words off-limits | BI7 |
| BI7 | Aftercare | How you want to come back | Aftercare map dot (with a second dot for the next day, optional); extras as icons (water, snack, blanket, comfort item); presence and privacy choices (video, others present, public scenes) | BI8 |
| BI8 | Review and share | See what your Top will see | Summary card of everything entered; edit any section; who can see this profile (per connection); Share button | BH1 |

## Scene day and after

During the scene the bottom's phone is put away; signals are spoken. The app only asks for the bottom's attention before and after.

| ID | Screen | What it's for | Key elements | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BL1 | Pre-scene check | Tell your Top how you are today | Body today slider and tap on anything sore or new; emotional state slider and optional note; anything new since intake (yes or no); substance policy confirmed for today; which ingredients of your yes are present today; Send. Sending locks the consent snapshot | BL2 |
| BL2 | Scene in progress | Step out of the way | Calm screen confirming the check was sent; all Ember notifications muted until the scene ends | BA1 |
| BA1 | Debrief | Reflect in your own words | Opens a few hours after the scene ends, or on tap; how you feel now (slider); whether you felt seen and that you mattered; what differed from what you imagined and what to keep; one-tap replies ("Loved that, again please," "Yes to this, but another way," "That's a no for me now," "Something felt off, let's talk"); anything to change in your profile; skip any question | BH1 |
| BA2 | Feelings log | Catch drop early | A once-a-day tap for three days after an intense scene (one day after others); mood and energy sliders; if the log dips two days running, a gentle note about drop and one-tap buttons to message your Top or someone you trust | BC3 |

The Top's check-in, about 18 hours after the scene, arrives as a message in BC3.

## Profile and settings

The bottom owns their profile: they can see everything in it, change it anytime, and decide which Tops see what.

| ID | Screen | What it's for | Key elements | Leads to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BP1 | My profile | Everything you've shared, in one place | Sections: signals, essentials, desires (word groups, map, yes / maybe / no list), limits, aftercare, presence and privacy; each editable; "retake" for the yes / maybe / no list; date each section was last updated | BI screens |
| BP2 | History | How you've grown | Past scenes with your debriefs; changes to your limits and preferences, dated; overrides your Top made, with their dates | BP1 |
| BP3 | Sharing | Who sees what | Per connection: full profile, essentials only, or paused; revoke access in one tap | BP1 |
| BM1 | Me | Settings and account | Discreet mode and PIN; notifications (daily tips on by default with an opt-out, debrief and feelings log reminders); role toggle; export or delete my data; support Ember | BP1 |

**Overrides are visible to the bottom in History**, after the scene. That keeps the plan a surprise beforehand while giving the bottom a full record afterward.
